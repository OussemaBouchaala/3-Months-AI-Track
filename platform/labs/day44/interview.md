# User interview notes

## User
Salma, rental agent at a small real estate agency in Sousse (4 agents). She answers client questions all day and would use the tool 30 to 50 times a day.

## Today's workflow
1. A client writes on WhatsApp or calls.
2. Salma searches the shared folder of PDFs (contracts, fee grid, rules) or asks the manager.
3. She replies by voice note or text, often from memory.
4. For a visit, she writes it in a paper agenda and confirms by WhatsApp.

## Pain points
- About 40 repeated questions a day on fees, deposits and documents.
- Finding a clause in a scanned contract takes 5 to 10 minutes.
- Two double-booked visits last month because the agenda was not updated.

## Their questions
- Kaddeh el commission ki nechri appart?
- Combien de mois de caution pour une location à l'année ?
- شنية الوثائق اللازمة باش نكري؟
- Est-ce que les animaux sont acceptés ?
- Can a foreigner buy an apartment in Sousse?
- نجم نبطل الزيارة ؟
- Le loyer peut augmenter de combien après un an ?
- Chkoun yekhles el plombier ki tetkasser el robinet?
- Vous êtes ouverts pendant Ramadan ?

## Documents and data
About 120 PDFs: 80 scanned lease contracts, the fee grid (Word), the agency rules (PDF, French and Arabic), and around 300 WhatsApp conversations. The manager agreed to share the rules and the fee grid; contracts only with names removed.

## Success looks like
"If I can paste the client's message and get the answer with the page of the document in 10 seconds, I use it every day. And if it books the visit in the shared agenda, even better."

## Constraints
Client names and phone numbers must not leave the agency. Internet is unstable, so it should work on a local machine. No budget for a paid subscription above 30 TND a month. Answers must be in the client's language.
